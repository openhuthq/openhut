Each conversation an agent has is a **thread**, and every thread shows up in the web app, whether it started there, in Discord, from a webhook or from a routine.

## Find a thread

Open an agent in the sidebar to see its threads under it. Each one shows where it came from: **Web**, the Discord channel (**#agents-humans**), the webhook's name or the routine's name, and when it last moved.

- Threads **waiting on you** come first and say so. The agent's row counts them ("1 waiting"). A webhook setup thread stops waiting once the webhook gets its first call, or when you press **Done** on its card.
- The sidebar shows up to three threads per agent, plus every one waiting on you. **Show all** opens the full list, newest first, with search and a filter for Web, Discord, Webhook and Routine.

## Reply from the web

Open any thread and type. If the thread started in Discord, the header says **Started in Discord #channel ↗**, and your reply also goes there: it shows **Sent to #channel** under your message.

- **Also posting to #channel** in the header means the agent's replies go there too.
- **Paused posting to #channel · Resume**: replies stay in the web app until you press **Resume**.
- A thread whose webhook runs in Discord continues there. The web app shows it but has no composer: **Open in Discord ↗** takes you to it.

## See who's here

A thread is live for everyone who has it open.

- The header shows the faces of the people in the thread and **N here**. Click it to see who is **Viewing** or **Typing**, and whether the agent is **Replying**, **Working** or **Idle**.
- **Sara is typing** shows above the message box while someone else types. In the sidebar the thread says so too, or **Answering…** while the agent replies.
- The agent's reply streams to everyone as it writes, with what it is doing right now, **Answering Mo and Ahmed** and **Live · 3 watching**. That includes replies to webhooks and routines.
- **Seen by** under your last message shows who has the thread open now.

## Notify me

**More › Notify me** on the agent's panel sets where the agent tells you about things it finds on its own:

- **Usually in**: one of the agent's channels, or **Only here in the web app**.
- **Rules**: plain words the agent follows, like "Post incidents and new Linear issues in #on-call. Everything else stays here."

When the agent tells you somewhere else, the thread shows a quiet line: **Notified #on-call**.
