---
title: Slack
description: Each agent is its own Slack app. It answers in the channels you pick.
icon: logo:slack
---


<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the app

On the agent, under **Channels**, **Add**, then Slack, then **Create the Slack app**. Slack opens with the agent's name and settings filled in. Pick your workspace and press **Create**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Paste two tokens

Paste each one into the same box. OpenHut sorts them by how they start and ticks each off.

- **App token** (`xapp-`): **Basic Information** › **App-Level Tokens** › **Generate Token and Scopes**, with `connections:write`.
- **Bot token** (`xoxb-`): **Install App** › **Install to** your workspace › **Allow**, then copy the **Bot User OAuth Token**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Pick its channels

Pick channels from the list. Everyone in them can mention the agent, and it says hi when it joins. A channel shared with another company gets a warning, since people there could use the agent too. Private channels and group DMs show up once you type `/invite @agent` in them.

</Step>
<Step>

### Give it its face (optional)

Drag the agent's picture from the sheet onto **Basic Information** › **App icon** in Slack.

</Step>
</Steps>

Open Slack again under **Channels** to change its channels any time.
