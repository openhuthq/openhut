<Shot src="slack" alt="The Connect to Slack dialog" />

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the app

On the agent's page, **Set up** next to Slack, then **Create the Slack app**. Slack opens with everything filled in.

</Step>
<Step>

### Paste two tokens

The **Bot token** (`xoxb-`) and the **App-level token** (`xapp-`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose who can message it

Only people you list, or anyone in the workspace. Then press **Connect**.

</Step>
</Steps>
