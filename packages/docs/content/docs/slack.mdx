title: Slack
description: Each agent is its own Slack app in your workspace. People DM it or mention it in a channel.

<Shot src="slack" alt="The Connect to Slack dialog with its two steps: create the Slack app, then paste the tokens" />

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the Slack app

On the agent's page, press **Set up** next to **Slack**, then **Create the Slack app**. Slack opens with everything
filled in. Pick your workspace, create the app and install it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Paste the tokens

Copy two tokens from Slack into the dialog:

- **Bot token**, from **OAuth & Permissions**. It starts with `xoxb-`.
- **App-level token**, from **Basic Information**, with the `connections:write` scope. It starts with `xapp-`.

</Step>
<Step>

### Say who can message it

Add your own Slack member ID (your profile, then **Copy member ID**). Then choose **Only people I list** or **Anyone
in the workspace**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect

Press **Connect**. In Slack, DM the agent or mention it in a channel.

</Step>
</Steps>

Messages from people who aren't allowed are dropped. The agent doesn't reply to them.
