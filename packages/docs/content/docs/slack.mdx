---
title: Slack
description: Each agent is its own Slack app. It answers in the channels you pick.
icon: logo:slack
---

<Shot src="slack" alt="The Connect to Slack dialog, picking channels from a dropdown" />

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the app

On the agent's page, **Set up** next to Slack, then **Create the Slack app**. Slack opens with everything filled in.

</Step>
<Step>

### Paste two tokens

The **Bot token** (`xoxb-`) and the **App-level token** (`xapp-`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Pick its channels

Press **Connect**, then pick channels from the list. Everyone in them can mention the agent. Private channels and group DMs show up once you type `/invite @agent` in them.

</Step>
<Step>

### Change them any time

The agent's page lists its channels. Remove one with its ×, or add one from the same list.

</Step>
</Steps>
