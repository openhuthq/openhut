title: Slack
description: Each agent is its own Slack app, with its face. It answers in the channels you pick.
icon: logo:slack

<Shot src="slack" alt="The Connect to Slack dialog: create the app, then paste its three tokens" />

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the app

On the agent, under **Talk to it on**, **Set up** next to Slack, then **Create the Slack app**. Slack opens with everything filled in.

</Step>
<Step>

### Paste three tokens

- **App token** (`xapp-`): **Basic Information** › **App-Level Tokens** › **Generate Token and Scopes**, with the `connections:write` scope.
- **Bot token** (`xoxb-`): **OAuth & Permissions** › **Install to** your workspace › **Allow**, then copy the **Bot User OAuth Token**.
- **Configuration token** (`xoxe.xoxp-`): on [Your Apps](https://api.slack.com/apps), **Your App Configuration Tokens** › **Generate Token**. OpenHut uses it once to set the app's icon to the agent's face, and doesn't keep it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Pick its channels

Press **Connect**, then pick channels from the list. Everyone in them can mention the agent. Private channels and group DMs show up once you type `/invite @agent` in them.

</Step>
<Step>

### Change them any time

Open Slack again under **Talk to it on** to see its channels. Remove one with its ×, or add one from the same list.

</Step>
</Steps>
