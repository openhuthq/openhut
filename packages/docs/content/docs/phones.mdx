---
title: Phones
description: Talk to your agents from the OpenHut app on your phone, from anywhere.
icon: hut
---

<Steps>
<Step>

### Install the app

Install OpenHut on your phone (iPhone or Android).

</Step>
<Step>

### Make a code

On your computer, **Settings › Devices › Add a phone**. A code shows up. It works once, for 5 minutes.

</Step>
<Step>

### Scan it

In the phone app, **Scan a code** and point the camera at it. Check that both screens show the same six numbers, then **Allow** it on your Mac.

</Step>
</Steps>

The phone reaches the agents on all your [computers](/computers) through OpenHut's relay, end-to-end encrypted. It works while your Mac sleeps, as long as the agent's computer is on. Face ID (or your screen lock) unlocks it.

To remove a phone, **Settings › Devices**, then **Remove** next to it. It stops reaching your agents right away.
