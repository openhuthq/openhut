---
title: Phones
description: Talk to your agents from the OpenHut app on your phone, from anywhere.
icon: hut
---

<Steps>
<Step>

### Install the app

Install OpenHut on your phone (iPhone or Android).

</Step>
<Step>

### Sign in

Sign in with the same Google account you use on your computer, then tap **Request access**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Approve it on your Mac

A notice on your Mac says the phone wants to join. **Approve** it there, or in **Settings › Computers**. Check the phone's name before you approve. A request expires after an hour.

</Step>
</Steps>

The phone reads your agents and chats from [OpenHut Cloud](/cloud) and decrypts them itself, so they're there while your Mac sleeps. Talking to an agent needs the agent's [computer](/computers) on: your message reaches it through OpenHut's relay, end-to-end encrypted. Face ID (or your screen lock) unlocks the app. The phone never holds your connection or API keys.

To remove a phone, **Settings › Computers**, then **Remove** next to it. It's cut off right away and wipes itself.
