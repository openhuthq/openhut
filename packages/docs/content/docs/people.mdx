## Invite someone

**Settings › People › Invite**. Pick **Member** or **Admin**, then **Copy invite link** and send it yourself. Each link works once, for 7 days. Unused links are under **Pending invites**, where **Revoke** cancels one.

## Roles

- **Owner:** the first account, made at [install](/install). Can't be changed or removed.
- **Admin:** also invites people, adds computers, connects models and changes settings.
- **Member:** chats with the agents the team shares, and makes their own.

## Shared with team

Agents are private to their owner until they're **Shared with team**. Then everyone can chat with them. See [Agents](/agents).

## Change or remove someone

Open their menu: **Make admin**, **Make member**, or **Remove from team**. A removed person is signed out and can't sign in again. Their agents stay.
