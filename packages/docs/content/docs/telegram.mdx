---
title: Telegram
description: Each agent is its own Telegram bot. DM it or mention it in a group.
icon: logo:telegram
---


<Steps>
<Step>

### Make the bot

On the agent, under **Channels**, **Add**, then Telegram, then **Open BotFather**. Send `/newbot`, give it a name, then a username ending in `bot`. Paste the token BotFather sends and press **Next**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Make it yours

Open the bot from the sheet, or scan the code with your phone, and tap **Start**. Whoever taps first becomes the owner, and the link works for 10 minutes.

</Step>
<Step>

### Invite anyone else

**Copy invite link** and send it. When they tap **Start**, they're in and show up in the list, where you can remove them. Each link works once, for 24 hours.

</Step>
</Steps>

In a group the agent answers when it is mentioned. If it stays silent there, turn off privacy mode in BotFather (`/setprivacy`), then remove the bot from the group and add it again.
