<Steps>
<Step>

### Pick one

**Connections**, then the service. A short guide opens with what agents get and the steps.

</Step>
<Step>

### Paste a key or sign in

Postgres, Convex and a custom MCP server take a key. PostHog, Linear and Google open their sign-in. Google needs an OAuth client once, in **Admin**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Hand it to an agent

Tick it under **Connections** on the agent's page.

</Step>
<Step>

### See what it can do and did

Open the connection: its tools, and every call, refused ones too.

</Step>
</Steps>

Postgres queries are one `SELECT`, at most 15 seconds and 1000 rows. **Tailscale** joins this server to your tailnet so connections reach private hosts; agents stay off it. Turn on **Everyone on the team** to share a connection.
