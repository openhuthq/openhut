<Steps>
<Step>

### Pick one

**Connect apps**, at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Connect** next to the service. A short guide opens with the steps.

An agent that needs an app asks for it in chat, with a **Connect** button that opens the same guide.

</Step>
<Step>

### Paste a key or sign in

Postgres, Convex and a custom MCP server take a key. PostHog, Linear, GitHub and Google open their sign-in. Google signs in through OpenHut Cloud, which holds OpenHut's Google app and passes the tokens straight back to your computer. Google shows a warning that the app isn't verified yet: choose **Advanced**, then **Go to OpenHut**. Excalidraw takes neither: agents draw a diagram and send a link to it, and anyone with the link can open it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Hand it to an agent

On the agent, **Add** it under **Can use**. Only an agent's owner can, and only with their own connections.

</Step>
</Steps>

A connection belongs to your account, not to one computer: every computer of yours can use it. Its key opens only on your computers. Your browsers see its name, never its key, and teammates see neither.

Open a connection to see its tools and every call, refused ones too.

Postgres queries are one `SELECT`, at most 15 seconds and 1000 rows. Agents can also see a query's plan with `EXPLAIN`, never `EXPLAIN ANALYZE`, so the query doesn't run.
