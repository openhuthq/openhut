<Steps>
<Step>

### Pick one

**Connect apps**, at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Connect** next to the service. A short guide opens with the steps.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose its computers

Under **Computers**, pick where it goes: this Mac, a [computer](/computers) you added, or both. Its key stays on each computer you pick.

</Step>
<Step>

### Paste a key or sign in

Postgres, Convex and a custom MCP server take a key. PostHog, Linear, GitHub and Google open their sign-in. Google signs in through OpenHut Cloud, which holds OpenHut's Google app and passes the tokens straight back to your computer without keeping them. Google shows a warning that the app isn't verified yet: choose **Advanced**, then **Go to OpenHut**. Excalidraw takes neither: agents draw a diagram and send a link to it, and anyone with the link can open it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Hand it to an agent

On the agent, **Add** it under **Can use**. If it isn't on the agent's computer yet, **Add to** that computer.

</Step>
</Steps>

Open a connection to see its tools and every call, refused ones too.

Postgres queries are one `SELECT`, at most 15 seconds and 1000 rows.
