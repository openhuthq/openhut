---
title: Invite your team
description: People join by invite link. Each person builds and owns their own agents.
icon: team
---

<Shot src="people" alt="The People page listing the owner, an admin and a member" />

<Steps>
<Step>

### Make a link

Open **People**, press **Invite person** and pick a role: **Member** or **Admin**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Send it

The link works once and for 7 days. The person opens it and picks a username and password.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Removing someone

Press **Remove** next to their name. They can't sign in any more, their assistants are disconnected and their agents
stop.
