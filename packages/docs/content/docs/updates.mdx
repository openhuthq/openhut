---
title: Updates
description: One click updates the whole install, and a failed update rolls back.
icon: support
---

**Settings › Updates**. When **An update is ready**, press **Update now**. **What's new** links to the [changelog](https://openhut.dev/changelog).

OpenHut restarts once and agents pick up where they left off. Your main install updates first; your other [computers](/computers) follow it to the same version.

If the new version doesn't start, OpenHut goes back to the previous one with your data as it was, and shows **Try again**.

Only owners and admins can update.
