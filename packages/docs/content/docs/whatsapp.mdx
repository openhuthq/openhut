---
title: WhatsApp
description: Each agent answers on its own WhatsApp number, linked the same way as WhatsApp Web.
icon: logo:whatsapp
---

<Shot src="whatsapp" alt="The Connect to WhatsApp dialog asking for your phone number and who can message" />

You need a phone with WhatsApp on the number the agent will use. A spare SIM works well.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Say who can message it

On the agent's page, press **Set up** next to **WhatsApp**. Enter your own number with the country code, then choose
**Only numbers I list** or **Anyone who messages the number**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Scan the QR code

Press **Link**. On the agent's phone, open WhatsApp, go to **Linked devices** and scan the code on screen.

</Step>
<Step>

### Message it

Send the number a message from your own phone. The agent answers.

</Step>
</Steps>
