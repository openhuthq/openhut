<Shot src="whatsapp" alt="The Connect to WhatsApp dialog" />

You need a phone with WhatsApp on the agent's number. A spare SIM works.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Choose who can message it

On the agent's page, **Set up** next to WhatsApp. Only numbers you list, or anyone.

</Step>
<Step>

### Scan the QR code

Press **Link**. On the agent's phone, open **Linked devices** and scan.

</Step>
</Steps>
