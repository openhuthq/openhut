---
title: WhatsApp
description: Each agent answers on its own number, linked like WhatsApp Web.
icon: logo:whatsapp
---


You need a phone with WhatsApp on the agent's number. A spare SIM works.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Choose who can message it

In the agent's **Setup › Channels**, **Connect** WhatsApp. Enter your number, then only numbers you list, or anyone. If the agent asked for WhatsApp in a chat, **Approve** its card, then **Scan the QR code**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Scan the QR code

On the agent's phone, open **Settings › Linked devices › Link a device** and scan.

</Step>
</Steps>
