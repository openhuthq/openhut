title: WhatsApp
description: Each agent answers on its own number, linked like WhatsApp Web.
icon: logo:whatsapp
---

<Shot src="whatsapp" alt="The Connect to WhatsApp dialog" />

You need a phone with WhatsApp on the agent's number. A spare SIM works.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Choose who can message it

On the agent, under **Talk to it on**, **Set up** next to WhatsApp. Enter your number, then only numbers you list, or anyone.

</Step>
<Step>

### Scan the QR code

Press **Show the code**. On the agent's phone, open **Settings › Linked devices › Link a device** and scan.

</Step>
</Steps>
