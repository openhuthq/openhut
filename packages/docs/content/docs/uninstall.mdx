## The app

Stop your agents and quit OpenHut, then move it to the Trash (on Linux, delete the AppImage, or remove the .deb package).

To delete its agents and data too, remove its folder: `~/Library/Application Support/OpenHut` on a Mac, `~/.config/OpenHut` on Linux. **Settings › General** shows where it is. This can't be undone.

## A computer you added

In the app, **Settings › Your devices**, then **⋯ › Remove**. Its agents stop, and nothing on it is deleted.

To wipe it, run this on the computer:

```sh
docker rm -f openhut && docker volume rm openhut
```

This removes OpenHut, every agent there and all its data. It can't be undone. Docker stays installed.
