---
title: Discord
description: Each agent is its own Discord bot. It answers in the channels you pick.
icon: logo:discord
---

<Steps>
<Step>

### Make the bot

On the agent, under **Channels**, **Add**, then Discord, then **Open Discord**. **New Application**, give it the agent's name. On **Bot**, turn **Public Bot** off and **Message Content Intent** on, then **Reset Token** and copy it. Paste it in the sheet and press **Next**. OpenHut checks both switches before it goes on.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add it to your server

Under **Add it to your server**, open the link. Discord opens with the right permissions already ticked: pick the server and **Authorize**. The sheet notices when it joins.

</Step>
<Step>

### Pick its channels

Pick its channels from the list and press **Connect**. A private channel shows a lock. If the bot isn't in it yet, open the channel's settings in Discord, then **Permissions**, and add the bot; the list picks it up when you open it again. It says hi in each one. Everyone there can mention it, and it answers in a thread. It doesn't answer DMs.

</Step>
</Steps>

Open Discord again under **Channels** to change its channels or add it to another server.
