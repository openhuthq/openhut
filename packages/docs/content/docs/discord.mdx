---
title: Discord
description: Each agent is its own Discord bot. It answers in the channels you pick.
icon: logo:discord
---

<Steps>
<Step>

### Make the bot

On the agent, under **Talk to it on**, **Set up** next to Discord, then **Open Discord**. **New Application**, give it the agent's name. On **Bot**, turn **Public Bot** off and **Message Content Intent** on, then **Reset Token** and copy it. Paste it in the sheet and press **Next**. OpenHut checks both switches before it goes on.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add it to your server

Press **Add the agent to a server**. Discord opens with the right permissions already ticked: pick the server and **Authorize**. The sheet notices when it joins.

</Step>
<Step>

### Pick its channels

Tick its channels from the list and press **Connect**. It says hi in each one. Everyone there can mention it, and it answers in a thread. It doesn't answer DMs.

</Step>
</Steps>

Open Discord again under **Talk to it on** to change its channels or add it to another server.
