---
title: Discord
description: Each agent is its own Discord bot. It answers in the channels you list.
icon: logo:discord
---

<Steps>
<Step>

### Make a bot

On the [Discord Developer Portal](https://discord.com/developers/applications), **New Application** and give it the agent's name. On **Bot**, turn on **Message Content Intent** and **Save Changes**. OpenHut checks it's on: without it Discord drops the bot's connection.

</Step>
<Step>

### Invite it to your server

On **OAuth2** › **URL Generator**, tick `bot`, then under Bot Permissions tick **View Channels**, **Send Messages**, **Send Messages in Threads**, **Create Public Threads** and **Read Message History**. Open the link at the bottom and pick your server. For a private channel, also add the bot to it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Copy the token and the channel IDs

- **Bot token**: **Bot** › **Reset Token**, then copy it.
- **Channel IDs**: in Discord, **User Settings** › **Advanced** › turn on **Developer Mode**. Then right-click a channel, like `#on-call`, › **Copy Channel ID**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect

On the agent, under **Talk to it on**, **Set up** next to Discord. Paste the token and the channel IDs and press **Connect**. Everyone in those channels can mention the agent; it answers in a thread. It doesn't answer DMs.

</Step>
<Step>

### Change them any time

Open Discord again under **Talk to it on** to change its channel IDs.

</Step>
</Steps>
