<Shot src="models" alt="Settings, Models: the Claude subscription and ChatGPT" />

<Steps>
<Step>

### Sign in

**Settings › Models**, then **Sign in** next to **ChatGPT subscription**. The app shows a code.

</Step>
<Step>

### Enter the code

Open the page it links to, sign in to ChatGPT and enter the code. The app finishes on its own.

</Step>
<Step>

### Pick it on an agent

On the agent, **Model** lists the ChatGPT models next to the Claude ones.

</Step>
</Steps>

It needs ChatGPT Plus or Pro. Use a plan set aside for agents: their work counts against its limits.
