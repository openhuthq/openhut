---
title: Multiple agents
description: One agent per job. Give it a name, a job, and what it can reach.
icon: ops
---

<Shot src="agent" alt="An agent's page: instructions, status, channels and connections" />

<Steps>
<Step>

### Name it

**Agents**, then **New agent**. Name it after its job: Support, Sales, Dev.

</Step>
<Step>

### Tell it its job

Write its **Instructions** the way you'd brief a new teammate. Two sentences are enough.

</Step>
<Step>

### Give it a channel and data

Set up [Slack](/slack) or [WhatsApp](/whatsapp), and tick the [data](/data) it needs.

</Step>
<Step>

### Start it

Press **Start**. **Stop** pauses it and keeps what it learned.

</Step>
</Steps>
