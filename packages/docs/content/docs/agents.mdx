<Shot src="agent" alt="The Dev agent's page: instructions on the left, status, channels and connections on the right" />

<Steps>
<Step>

### Name it

Open **Agents** and press **New agent**. Name it after its job: Support, Sales, Dev. You own the agents you create.

</Step>
<Step>

### Tell it its job

Press **Open to edit** under **Instructions** and write what it does, the way you'd brief a new teammate. Two or
three sentences are enough to start. Saving restarts the agent.

```md title="Instructions"
# Support

Answer customers in #support. Look the customer up in the Orders DB before you reply.
If you aren't sure, say so and ask a teammate.
```

</Step>
<Step>

### Give it a place to talk

Under **Channels**, set up [Slack](/slack), [WhatsApp](/whatsapp) or both.

</Step>
<Step>

### Give it data

Under **Connections**, tick the ones it needs. It can read those and nothing else. No connections yet?
[Add your data](/data).

</Step>
<Step>

### Start it

Press **Start**. The status turns to **Active** and the agent answers in its channels. **Stop** pauses it and keeps
everything it has learned.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Pick a model

**Model** on the agent's page switches between Sonnet, Opus and Haiku. Sonnet is a good default.
