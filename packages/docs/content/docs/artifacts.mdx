Ask an agent for a report, a chart or a page you can send someone:

```txt
Make a page that shows this week's order sync failures by store.
```

It answers with a card in the chat. The card's colour and icon say what it is (a page, a PDF, an image, notes or a file), and the small lock, people or link icon says who can open it.

## What an agent can make

- **Pages**: HTML with its own scripts, styles and images. A page runs in a sandbox, so it can't use your sign-in or reach OpenHut.
- **PDFs** open in your browser's own PDF viewer.
- **Images** and **notes** (Markdown) show beside the chat.
- **Other files**, like a CSV, a spreadsheet or a zip, download when you click the card.

Each holds up to 200 files and 10 MB. To change one, ask the agent: it reads what's there, edits it and publishes it again. If someone else changed it in the meantime, the agent reads it again first, so nobody's edit is lost.

## Opening and sharing

Click the card and it opens on the right of the chat. Drag the line between them to make either side wider (the chat keeps at least 420px), double-click the line to go back to half and half, or focus it and use the arrow keys. OpenHut remembers the width, and the address changes too, so reload and the back button bring it back. On a phone, or in a narrow window, it opens full screen; a PDF opens in a new tab.

Everyone who can use the agent can open, share and delete what it makes. **Share** at the top shows who has access:

- **Add people by email** lets someone on your team view it without giving them the agent.
- **General access** is **Private** (only the people listed), **Team** (anyone signed in to your team) or **Anyone with link** (no sign-in). With a link, **Copy** copies it and **Reset link** makes a new one, so the old link stops working.

A link opens the page on its own, with a thin bar that says which agent made it. You can also ask the agent to share it; it only shares when someone in the chat asks.

If someone loses access to the agent, they lose what it made at the same moment.

## Delete it

The **⋯** menu has **Open in new tab**, **Copy link** and **Delete**. Delete asks once, right there, then removes it for everyone, links included.
