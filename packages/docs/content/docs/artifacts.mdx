Ask an agent for a report, a chart or a page you can send someone, and it answers with a card in the chat. The card's colour and icon say what it is (a page, a PDF, an image, notes or a file), and the small lock, people or link icon says who can open it.

## Open it beside the chat

Click the card and it opens on the right of the chat. Drag the line between them to make either side wider (the chat keeps at least 420px), double-click the line to go back to half and half, or focus it and use the arrow keys. OpenHut remembers the width. The address changes too, so reload and the back button bring it back.

- A page runs in a sandbox: it can't use your sign-in or reach OpenHut.
- A PDF opens in your browser's own viewer. On a phone it opens in a new tab.
- A file OpenHut can't show, like a spreadsheet or a zip, downloads when you click its card.

On a phone, or in a narrow window, it opens full screen instead.

## Share it

Everyone who can use the agent can open, share and delete its pages. **Share** at the top of the page shows who has access:

- **Add people by name or email** lets someone view it without giving them the agent.
- **General access** is **Private** (only the people listed), **Team** (anyone signed in to your team) or **Anyone with link** (no sign-in). With a link, **Copy** copies it and **Reset link** makes a new one, so the old link stops working.

A link opens the page on its own, with a thin bar that says which agent made it.

## Delete it

The **⋯** menu has **Open in new tab**, **Copy link** and **Delete**. Delete asks once, right there, then removes it for everyone, links included.
