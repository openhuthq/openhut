<Steps>
<Step>

### Download it

- **Mac:** [Download for Mac](https://openhut.dev/download/mac). Apple silicon, macOS 14 or later. Open the dmg and drag OpenHut to Applications.
- **Linux:** [Download the AppImage](https://openhut.dev/download/linux), or the [.deb](https://github.com/openhuthq/openhut/releases/latest/download/openhut-x64.deb) for Debian and Ubuntu (`sudo apt install ./openhut-x64.deb`). x64 with KVM.

</Step>
<Step>

### Open it

There's no sign-in and no account. The first start takes a minute or two while it gets ready; after that it opens in about ten seconds.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add your Claude plan

**Settings › Models**, then **Sign in** next to Claude subscription. See [Claude plan](/claude).

</Step>
<Step>

### Make an agent

**+** at the top of the sidebar. Pick a face and a name, then **Create**. Tell it its job under **What it does**. See [Multiple agents](/agents).

</Step>
<Step>

### Add a computer (optional)

Agents on your Mac answer only while it's awake. For agents that stay on, add an always-on Linux computer from **Settings › Computers**. See [Computers](/computers).

</Step>
</Steps>

Each agent runs in its own small virtual machine on your computer, with about 1 GB of memory.

## Keep your Mac awake

**Settings › General**:

- **Keep this Mac awake while agents run.** The screen can still turn off. A closed lid or a sleeping Mac still stops them.
- **Open at login.** Agents start in the background, without the window.

Closing the window on a Mac keeps agents running. On Linux it quits the app; the agents keep running and the app finds them when you open it again.

## Troubleshooting

- **"This computer can't run agents" on Linux.** The app needs KVM (`/dev/kvm`). Turn on virtualization (Intel VT-x or AMD-V) in the firmware settings, or nested virtualization in a VM. Then let your user open it: `sudo usermod -aG kvm $USER`, and sign in again.
- **Intel Mac.** Not supported: the Mac app needs Apple silicon. Use the Linux app on an x64 Linux machine instead.
- **Agents stop answering at night.** Your Mac went to sleep. Turn on **Keep this Mac awake**, or move the agent to an always-on [computer](/computers).
- **No computers to add.** Tailscale must be installed and signed in on this Mac. See [Computers](/computers).
