OpenHut works without an account. Turn on OpenHut Cloud when you want backups, the same agents on every Mac, and a team. Turn it on in **Settings › Cloud** with your Google account.

## What we can't read

Your agents, their instructions, chats, memory, files and connection keys are locked on your Mac before they leave it. The key that opens them is made on your Mac and never reaches us. Without it, what we store is noise.

## What we can see

- Your email, from Google, so we know who signed in.
- Who is in your workspace: their emails, roles and devices. Your devices sign this list, so we can't change it.
- How much you store and when, to keep the service running.

We ask Google only for your email. We never see your Google password, and signing in doesn't give us your mail or files.

## Save a way back in

We can't reset your key. When you turn the cloud on, OpenHut asks you to save a recovery kit: 12 words. With your Google account, they unlock your cloud on a new Mac. Keep them offline, like a password.

To make a new kit, **Settings › Cloud › Make a new kit**. The old words stop working once you've checked the new ones.

## Sign out

**Settings › Cloud › Sign out**. Your agents keep running on your Mac, and your phone keeps working. Sign in again with the same Google account to pick up where you left off. A Mac holds one account's keys at a time, so it asks for the account it used before.
