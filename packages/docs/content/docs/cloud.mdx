You sign in to OpenHut Cloud with **Continue with Google**. It keeps your agents, chats and connections the same on every device you sign in on, and shares agents with your team. Records are sealed before they're stored; we keep a copy of your account's key, so any browser you sign in on gets in with no recovery file to keep.

## What's in your account

- Your agents: name, instructions, model, face, where each one runs and whether it's on.
- Chats, memory, skills and routines.
- What each agent can use, and its channels, with their tokens.
- Connections and API keys. One connection works for your whole account: every computer of yours can use it, with no copy per computer.
- Files you add to a chat.
- Your settings, like the models you switched on and your time zone.

Your devices fetch what they need and open it themselves. A browser keeps nothing. A computer keeps only what its own agents need to run, so they keep answering in Slack and Telegram, and routines keep running, even when the cloud is down. The app then shows **Offline** and doesn't take changes until it's back.

## What stays on the agent's computer

An agent's own files on the computer that runs it aren't in your account: its home and work files, its browser logins, the WhatsApp link and the working database it keeps for itself. Move an agent to another computer and these stay behind, so link WhatsApp again there. Deleting a computer's OpenHut data deletes them for good.

## Who can open what

- **Your private agents**, with their chats and memory, and the names of your connections: all your devices, browsers included.
- **Connection keys, API keys and channel tokens**: only your computers. Browsers never get them, and teammates never do.
- **Shared agents**, with their chats and memory: your teammates' devices too.

Only an agent's owner can change what it can use, and only with their own connections. An agent runs only on its owner's computers.

## What we can see

- Your email, from Google, so we know who signed in.
- Who is in each team: emails, roles, and devices with their names.
- The shape of your data: how many agents, messages and other records you have and how big they are.
- When your devices are online, and when someone in a team is typing.

We keep a copy of your account's key so a new browser gets in with just your Google sign-in. We ask Google only for your email; we never see your Google password, and signing in doesn't give us your mail or files. When you connect a Google app, the sign-in and its token refreshes pass through OpenHut Cloud, because Google requires it.

## A new device

Sign in with the same Google account, and you're in. A server joins with the one-use code from **Add a computer** instead of a Google sign-in: see [Computers](/computers).

## Not me

If a device on your account isn't yours, remove it in **Settings › Computers**. It's cut off right away.

## Teams

Everyone has a team of their own. To share it, **Settings › Team › Invite**: pick an email and a role, then send the link. A link works once, for 48 hours, and only for that email.

Each agent is **Private** or **Shared**. Your teammates see and chat with Shared agents. Private ones stay yours; teammates can't open them.

## Removing someone

When you remove someone, or a device, the cloud refuses them from then on and they get nothing new. Anything they already read, they could have kept.

## Sign out

**Settings › Account › Sign out** signs this browser out. To use it again, sign in.

## Delete your account

**Settings › Account › Delete account**, at the bottom. It deletes everything OpenHut Cloud keeps for you: your agents, chats, connections and settings, your devices list and your sign-in. The cloud is the only copy, so this can't be undone. Your agents stop on every computer. Teams you own alone are deleted, and you leave the teams you joined. If you own a team with other people in it, make someone else its owner or delete it first.
