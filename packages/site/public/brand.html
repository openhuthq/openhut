Brand guide

A warm little hut on the sand, for your agents

Every agent gets its own sandbox to dig in. The hut keeps the lights on, on your own server. Below are the mark, the colours, the type and the crew, ready to copy.

The mark

Three huts, one family

Same hut every time: thatched roof, cream walls, a big kind face and a red flag. The shore and the badge add the beach only where there is room for it. Every size below is the same SVG drawn at real pixels.

Hut

Primary mark

Wordmark lockup, app chrome, avatars. Below 24 px it swaps to a small cut: no thatch lines, dot eyes, a bigger flag.

Hut on the shore

Stickers

Stickers, merch, the footer and empty states. Below 32 px the sand turns to noise, so use the small hut.

Beach badge

App icon

A whole little beach in a rounded square. Home screens, Slack app, social avatars.

Colour

Thatch, sand and a lagoon

The hut owns thatch and flag red. The beach brings sand, lagoon and deep water. Sunset is for highlights and the odd beach ball. Click a swatch to copy its hex.

Type

Round letters, like the crew

Fredoka matches the soft, thick-lined creatures and sets the wordmark. Figtree carries the reading. Plex Mono labels things, the way a hand-painted beach sign would.

Mascot

The crew, dressed for the beach

Same creature generator, same 4.5 outline, blink and breath. Costumes moved to beachwear: bucket hat, snorkel, sun hat, a flower lei. Hover any of them to make it hop.

For developers
  • The hut keeps the class hooks c-body, c-face, c-eyes, c-mouth and c-hat and the viewBox 4 8 100 100, so hop, blink, look and talk work unchanged.
  • The flag is the hut's c-hat, so it tilts on a hop like every other hat.
  • In dark mode the beach turns to night: a moon, stars, and a lantern glow around the hut.
Rules

Using the hut

A few rules keep the hut looking like itself everywhere it goes.

Clear space

Keep one flag height of empty space on every side. Nothing else goes inside that box.

Minimum sizes

Swap to the small hut at 24 px and below. Never show the shore version under 32 px.

Ink on Thatch 12.6 · AAA Ink on Lagoon 9.3 · AAA Ink on Flag 6.4 · AA Foam on Deep water 5.7 · AA Deep water on Sand 4.3 · large only Foam on Flag 3.0 · avoid

Text on colour

Buttons on Flag red take ink text, not white. Deep water on Sand is for headings only.

In use

On the sand and after dark
