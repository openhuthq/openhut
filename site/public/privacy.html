Privacy

Updated 25 November 2026

OpenHut is self-hosted. It runs on your own server, and your agents, chats, memory, connections, keys and accounts stay there. None of it reaches us. This page covers what does.

Your own services

Models, Google, Telegram, Slack and WhatsApp use your own keys, OAuth client, bots and apps. Your server talks to them directly; nothing passes through us. Their own privacy terms apply.

Usage data

The app sends no usage events. This website counts page views and install script downloads without cookies.

Removing access

Delete a connection in OpenHut, or remove OpenHut from your Google account’s third-party access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/openhuthq/openhut.
