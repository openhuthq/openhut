Privacy

Updated 23 November 2026

OpenHut runs your agents on your own computers, and you sign in to OpenHut Cloud to use it. This page covers what reaches us.

OpenHut Cloud

We store your agents, chats, memory, connections and settings, encrypted on your device before they reach us. No one can read them, including us. An agent’s own files on the computer that runs it, like its browser logins, stay there.

We can see your email, who is in each team, device names, how many records you store and how big they are, which agent each message belongs to, which device wrote it and when, and which computer runs each agent. We can’t see names, contents, which apps you connect or what each agent may use.

Settings › Account › Delete account in the app deletes what we store for you.

Google sign-in through OpenHut Cloud

You sign in to OpenHut with Google. We ask only for your email; we never see your Google password, and signing in doesn’t give us your mail or files.

When you connect Gmail, Drive, Calendar or another Google app, your browser goes to Google and back through cloud.openhut.dev/api, which holds OpenHut’s Google app. It exchanges the one-time code and refreshes tokens on your computer’s behalf, then hands the tokens straight back. Your computer encrypts them before they’re stored in OpenHut Cloud, so we can’t read them, and calls Google directly; your Google data never passes through us.

OpenHut’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don’t use Google user data for advertising, sell it, or use it to train models.

Usage data

The app sends no usage events. We count sign-ups and active devices from sign-ins. The website counts page views and downloads without cookies.

Removing access

Delete a connection in OpenHut, or remove OpenHut from your Google account’s third-party access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
